# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of **GLASTOR® Core** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at [ventas@glastor.es](mailto:ventas@glastor.es). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
